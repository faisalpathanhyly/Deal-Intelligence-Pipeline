## Method

Each candidate was run through the verification-rules.md checks. For each one I:
1. Traced the claim to its primary source (transcript cue, dated Notion field, or HubSpot property), and checked the verbatim wording.
2. Looked for a contradicting record in the same period.
3. Tried to disprove the claim with the most plausible innocent reading.
4. Checked whether the stated impact is supported or only plausible.
5. Narrowed the claim where step 3 or 4 failed, then tested the narrowed claim again.

Verdicts: APPROVED (condition level; impact not claimed) | NEEDS EVIDENCE | REJECTED as originally stated.

Source labels: [Transcript] verbatim speech, with cue and timestamp. [Record] a Notion field or page, with row or date. [HubSpot] a CRM property. [AI] AI-generated text in Notion; not evidence on its own, used only to point to a cue.
