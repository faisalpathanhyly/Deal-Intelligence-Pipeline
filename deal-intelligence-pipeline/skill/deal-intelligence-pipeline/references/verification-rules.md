# Verification Rules

Verification must challenge the analysis rather than rephrase it.

## Exact-Fact Verification

Specific names, emails, owners, dates, meeting hosts, assignments, commercial terms, contract facts, and technical facts require an opened, read, allowlisted source explicitly supporting the exact claim. Record the source ID/link, field/section/timestamp, and supporting excerpt. Do not infer an email from a name, ownership from attendance/title, a contract date from a meeting date, or an assignment from a template. Withhold unsupported exact facts. Keep unread evidence Not Retrieved rather than labeling the fact Unknown or Needs Evidence merely because it was not read; only reviewed/search-exhausted or inaccessible evidence may support unresolved analysis outcomes. Disclose conflicting versions. Human intake values remain human-provided identification, not independently verified source facts.

For each exact claim, retain claim/value, opened source, source location, supporting excerpt, and status (Retrieved, Not Retrieved, Inaccessible, Conflicting, or Not Found After Review). A title, snippet, rollup name, metadata reference, or empty template is not claim support. Check source authorization before using evidence.

## Phase 5 Signal Boundary

Only Phase 5 may turn a candidate into verified/approved reusable intelligence: perform the repository checks, present outcomes, and obtain `APPROVE PHASE 5`. Before that command, any recommended Approved outcome remains provisional. Factual checks in other phases do not promote signals; Phase 4 selection and Phase 6 storage cannot bypass Phase 5. Changed evidence reopens affected verification and approval.

Activity, enthusiasm, demo success, report creation, and another scheduled meeting are not progression without later evidence of a meaningful decision, commitment, or change. Another deal cannot supply exact facts or missing evidence for this deal.

## For Every Candidate Signal or Major Conclusion, Check

1. Is the source legitimate and approved?
2. Is the underlying fact explicitly supported?
3. Is interpretation being presented as fact?
4. Is correlation being confused with causation?
5. Is there later-outcome evidence supporting the claimed impact?
6. Is there contradictory evidence?
7. Is the conclusion too generic to be useful?
8. Could another explanation fit the evidence equally well?
9. Is the conclusion relying on a different deal to fill a gap?
10. Is the conclusion dependent on a missing fact that should become a pending question?

## Evidence Coverage Check

Confirm exhaustive review includes every in-scope transcript, recording, and database result, with reconciled counts and honest modality coverage. Reject completion claims based on summaries, sampling, or missing ledger entries; reopen retrieval and affected review gates.

For every deal, check the Phase 1 retrieval ledger and the evidence behind each assessed dimension or claimed gap. Confirm that relevant linked-record bodies, rollup sources, and meeting content were actually read; a parent summary or title/date inventory is insufficient.

Do not approve an affected conclusion or reusable signal while material supporting evidence is Not Retrieved or Inaccessible. Record Needs Evidence with the specific retrieval task or access limitation. Retrieve available relevant evidence and return it through source and factual review before re-verification. Unaffected supported findings may proceed through their normal gates.

For a claimed missing fact, require Not Found After Review with reviewed sources and search scope, or a documented unresolved conflict. Neither a blank template field nor an unread source proves that a deal lacks a condition. A claim that a condition is absent requires affirmative evidence supporting that absence.

## Repository and Phase Audit

Before an Approved outcome, check and record:
- Required rules were loaded through a valid Repo Control Check, or explicitly authorized loaded fallback; no unresolved governing-rule conflict remains.
- Relevant retrieval completed, material linked records followed, and material retrieval gaps resolved.
- Claim support is not only a title, search result, preview, rollup, or unsupported summary; high-impact summary claims were checked against available transcript/raw evidence.
- Every provider is allowlisted or explicitly approved; no unlisted source was silently used.
- Other deals and methodology/example files supplied no factual evidence for this deal.
- Activity was not mistaken for progression; later evidence supports the claimed change.
- The correct phase was completed and the previous phase has its exact approval recorded; Phase 3 and Phase 5 were not skipped.
- Each exact fact has direct opened-source support, and duplicate-record or stage-field conflicts were investigated.

Material incomplete retrieval blocks Approved conclusions. Factual checks elsewhere do not verify candidate signals; retain the Phase 5 boundary.

## Verification Status

### Approved
Use when:
- the evidence supports the conclusion;
- no material contradiction remains unresolved;
- the interpretation is appropriately bounded;
- the signal preserves its original deal context.

### Needs Evidence
Use when:
- the conclusion is plausible but not sufficiently supported;
- later-outcome evidence is missing;
- source conflict remains unresolved;
- a material fact is absent.

### Rejected
Use when:
- the conclusion is unsupported;
- it depends on invented or assumed facts;
- it confuses activity with progression;
- it overstates causality;
- it is too generic to be useful;
- the evidence contradicts it.

## Confidence Standard

### High
- directly stated or strongly evidenced;
- supported by later outcome where causality / movement is claimed;
- little or no unresolved contradiction.

### Medium
- strongly implied;
- evidence is incomplete but materially supportive;
- interpretation remains bounded.

### Low
- partial evidence;
- important inference required;
- missing current or historical context;
- meaningful contradiction unresolved.

Low-confidence items should not automatically become approved reusable signals.
