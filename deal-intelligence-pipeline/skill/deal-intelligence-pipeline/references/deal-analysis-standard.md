# Deal Analysis Standard

Apply the same standard to every deal. The answers may differ, but the analysis structure should remain consistent.

## Evidence Coverage Before Analysis

Apply `source-hierarchy.md` to every deal. Establish facts from approved retrieved content, including relevant linked records and meeting bodies. For each material unresolved field, retain its coverage status and evidence references: Not Retrieved, Inaccessible, Conflicting, or Not Found After Review with reviewed sources and search scope. Do not fill template blanks with claims about missing deal context. Unread content is retrieval work; complete available research before asking the human for discoverable facts.

## 1. Deal Context

Capture only explicit facts:
- product;
- stage / outcome;
- deal type;
- primary requirement;
- scope.

## 2. Starting Position

Describe where the deal stood before meaningful movement:
- open decision;
- blocker;
- pending proof;
- unresolved stakeholder issue;
- material dependency.

## 3. What Actually Moved It

Identify one or more evidence-backed events, proof points, stakeholder actions, decisions, or state changes that materially contributed to progression.

Do not use generic statements such as `good meeting` or `client liked the demo` unless later evidence shows actual movement.

Preferred validation:

`Pre-call context -> Call event -> Later outcome`

## 4. Critical Pre-Call Context

Identify 1–3 facts the rep needed before an important call, for example:
- stakeholder change;
- internal alternative;
- technical constraint;
- implementation history;
- prior objection;
- budget pressure;
- adoption issue;
- decision requirement.

## 5. Dig Factor

Classify how easy the critical context was to obtain:
- Easy to find
- Required digging
- Unknown

## 6. Decision Structure

Where supported, identify functional influence:
- champion;
- decision-maker;
- gatekeeper;
- technical validator;
- operational stakeholder;
- weakened champion;
- missing stakeholder.

Do not infer influence from title alone.

## 7. Proof Needed

Identify proof tied to a decision:
- sample;
- ROI;
- pilot result;
- integration proof;
- reporting proof;
- rollout plan;
- success criteria.

## 8. Stall Reason

Identify the evidence-backed underlying reason for a stall.

Separate:
- surface symptom;
- underlying cause.

If the cause cannot be supported, mark Unknown.

## 9. Brief Catchability

Classify whether a pre-call brief could reasonably have surfaced the warning signs:
- Yes
- Partially
- No

List the specific warning signs.

## 10. Technical Dependency

Capture only material technical conditions:
- PMS;
- CRM;
- integration;
- data availability;
- internal BI;
- implementation;
- reporting condition.

## 11. Commercial Dependency

Capture only material commercial conditions:
- budget;
- pricing;
- MSA;
- procurement;
- legal;
- rollout timing;
- commercial approval.

## 12. Outcome / Delta

Describe what changed after the important call or period:
- stage movement;
- commitment;
- pause;
- expansion;
- cancellation;
- no movement;
- other explicit outcome.

## 13. Transferable Pattern

Normalize only after the deal-specific analysis is approved.

Use one of:
- Stakeholder
- Proof / Value
- Technical
- Commercial
- Adoption
- Risk / Blocker

## 14. Evidence Source

List the specific source supporting each major conclusion.

## 15. Confidence

Assign High, Medium, or Low using `verification-rules.md`.
