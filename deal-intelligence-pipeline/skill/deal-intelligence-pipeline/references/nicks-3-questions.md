# Nick's 3 Questions

Answer these explicitly for every closed deal.

If evidence is insufficient for any question, return `Unknown — insufficient evidence` and identify what would be needed.

## Question 1 — What actually moved it?

### Purpose
Identify what materially changed the deal state or created meaningful progression.

### Look For
- decision;
- commitment;
- stakeholder action;
- proof acceptance;
- commercial movement;
- rollout action;
- stage movement;
- technical validation tied to progression;
- measurable next step tied to progression.

### Required Evidence Pattern
Prefer:

`Pre-call context -> Call event -> Later outcome`

### Reject Weak Answers
Do not treat these alone as movement:
- positive feedback;
- feature interest;
- another meeting scheduled;
- successful demo;
- sample shown;
- client attendance.

### Output
- Answer
- Supporting evidence
- Later-outcome evidence
- Confidence

---

## Question 2 — What did the rep know going into that call that made the difference? Was it something they had to dig for?

### Purpose
Identify critical context that changed how the rep could prepare or act.

### Look For
- stakeholder change;
- decision ownership;
- internal alternative;
- technical constraint;
- previous implementation issue;
- prior objection;
- commercial pressure;
- budget concern;
- adoption issue;
- success criteria;
- proof requirement.

### Dig Factor
Assign one:
- Easy to find
- Required digging
- Unknown

### Output
- Critical pre-call context
- Why it mattered
- Where it came from
- Dig Factor
- Confidence

---

## Question 3 — When the deal stalled, what was the real reason, and would a brief have caught it?

### Purpose
Identify the underlying reason for stall and whether earlier warning signs were available.

### Method
1. identify visible symptom;
2. identify evidence-backed underlying cause;
3. list earlier warning signs;
4. assess whether a brief could have surfaced them.

### Brief Catchability
Assign one:
- Yes
- Partially
- No

### Output
- Stall symptom
- Underlying reason
- Supporting evidence
- Earlier warning signs
- Brief Catchability
- Confidence
